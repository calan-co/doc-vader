This guide provides a full, step-by-step process and best practices for researching, drafting, and maintaining user personas.

## Steps to Create User Personas

1. **Determine project goals**: Identify what your project aims to accomplish and how personas will support those goals.
2. **Survey existing user research**: Gather any available user feedback, data, or research from your organization or stakeholders.
3. **Draft proto-personas**: Brainstorm initial user types and their key attributes based on your goals and available data.
4. **Plan and conduct user research**: Validate proto-personas with real user interviews, surveys, or usability studies. Use both qualitative and quantitative methods where possible.
5. **Analyze results**: Look for patterns and insights in your research to refine your personas.
6. **Revise personas**: Update and complete persona details, removing or merging as needed.
7. **Get feedback**: Share personas with stakeholders and users for validation and improvement.
8. **Maintain personas**: Update personas as your understanding of users evolves or as project needs change.

## Best Practices

- Use real data where possible; avoid assumptions.
- Focus on user goals, needs, and pain points.
- Keep personas concise, actionable, and relevant to your project.
- Update personas regularly as you learn more about your users.
- Link each persona to specific project goals or features.

User personas are archetypal representations of users, designed to communicate user needs, motivations, and behaviors to teams. They help ensure that products and documentation are tailored to real user requirements.

## Why Use User Personas?

- Facilitate communication among teams
- Guide product and feature development
- Prioritize features based on user needs
- Improve usability and user experience

## Theoretical Background

Personas are grounded in user research and are most effective when based on real data. They serve as a tool for empathy and alignment across stakeholders.
